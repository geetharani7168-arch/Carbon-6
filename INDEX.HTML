📖 Chapter Overview

What is a Chemical Reaction?

Understanding the transformation of matter at the atomic and molecular level

🔬

Chemical Change

A process where reactants transform into new substances (products) with different properties. New bonds form and old bonds break.

⚖️

Chemical Equation

A symbolic representation using chemical formulas. A balanced equation obeys the Law of Conservation of Mass.

🔍

Signs of Chemical Reaction

  • Change in colour
  • Evolution of gas
  • Formation of precipitate
  • Change in temperature
  • Change in state

⚖️ Balancing a Chemical Equation

Law of Conservation of Mass: atoms of each element must be equal on both sides.

Unbalanced
Mg+O₂→MgO

O: 2 ≠ 1 ✗

Balanced ✓
2Mg+O₂→2MgO

Mg: 2=2 ✓   O: 2=2 ✓

🧠 Key Concepts

Oxidation, Reduction & Redox

Understanding electron transfer and oxygen gain/loss

🔥

Oxidation

  • Gain of oxygen
  • Loss of hydrogen
  • Loss of electrons
  • Substance = Reducing Agent
2Mg + O₂ → 2MgOMg is oxidised
⚡

Redox Reactions

Oxidation and reduction occur simultaneously. The oxidised substance is the reducing agent; the reduced substance is the oxidising agent.

CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂OCu²⁺ reduced; H₂ oxidised
❄️

Reduction

  • Loss of oxygen
  • Gain of hydrogen
  • Gain of electrons
  • Substance = Oxidising Agent
CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂OCuO is reduced
🦺

Corrosion

Gradual eating away of metals by air, moisture and other gases.

4Fe + 3O₂ + xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂ORust (hydrated iron oxide)
🛡️ Prevention: Painting, Galvanising, Alloying
🧈

Rancidity

Oxidation of fats/oils in food giving an unpleasant smell and taste. Caused by oxygen, moisture, and light.

🛡️ Prevention: Antioxidants, Nitrogen flushing, Refrigeration
