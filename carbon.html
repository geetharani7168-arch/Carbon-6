Why does soap not work in hard water?

Hard water contains Calcium (Ca²⁺) and Magnesium (Mg²⁺) salts.

When soap is added to hard water, it reacts with these ions to form an insoluble precipitate called Scum. This makes it difficult to form lather and clean clothes.

Note: Detergents are used to overcome this problem as they do not form insoluble precipitates in hard water.
